Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about replacing your lost earbud.

How do I replace a single lost earbud?

Simply find your earbud model on our site, select whether you need the left or right bud, and place your order. Most single-ear replacements pair automatically with your existing earbud out of the box. If you need help with pairing, check our setup guides or contact support.

Are your replacement parts genuine?

Yes, all our replacement parts are sourced from certified suppliers and meet original equipment quality standards. We thoroughly test every part before shipping to ensure perfect compatibility and performance.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 5-10 business days domestically and 10-20 business days internationally. Express shipping options are available at checkout for faster delivery. You will receive a tracking number once your order ships.

What if my earbud model isn't listed?

We're constantly adding new models. If you don't see your earbud model, contact us and we'll do our best to source the part you need. We typically add new models within 2-3 weeks of request.

Do you sell charging cases separately?

Yes, we offer individual charging case replacements for most major earbud models. Browse our catalog by brand to find the charging case that matches your device.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy on all unused items. If the part is defective or incompatible, we'll provide a full refund or replacement. Contact our support team to initiate a return.
